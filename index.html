How it works

Three steps from “I need one” to done

CampusKit runs from staffed pickup hubs on campus. You reserve online, collect in person and return the item when your rental ends.

Good to know

The rental rules, in plain words

Refundable deposit

Each item has a small deposit, paid at pickup and returned in full when the item comes back in the same condition.

Condition check

Hub staff check every item with you at pickup and return, so you are never charged for wear that was already there.

Extensions

Need it longer? Ask at the hub before your return date and extend if nobody else has reserved the item.

Late returns

Late items are charged at the daily rate, so the next student who reserved it is not left waiting before an exam.

About us

Built by students, for students who need the right tool for one semester

A first-year anatomy course, a second-year circuits lab, a final-year art portfolio: each one asks students to own equipment they use for a few weeks and then store for years.

CampusKit is a shared shelf for that equipment. We keep a stock of tools that medical, engineering and art students need for specific courses, check and clean them between rentals, and lend them out at a fraction of the purchase price.

The idea started as a university entrepreneurship project after we added up what our own classmates spent on tools they used for one course. Many students borrowed from friends, waited for a lab slot, or simply went without and fell behind.

Save money

Pay only for the weeks you use an item. A one-week rental usually costs only a fraction of buying the same tool new.

Access the right tools

Get course-grade equipment, from microscopes to drawing tablets, that would be hard to justify buying on a student budget.

Avoid academic delays

Reserve ahead of practicals and deadlines so a missing tool never holds back an exam, a lab report or a final project.
